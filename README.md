So hi there, this is a project I am trying to make more people realize the beauty of Mars, a planet which I have found amazing with its beautiful desert-like structures and vast landscape. I am really fascinated with space and what's out there, and Mars is one of the main reasons. Out of all the planets, I find Mars the most likely candidate to allow humans to become an inter-planetary species. So all these reasons (I'll quit yapping now, sorry) are what make me want to make a game which can spark an interest in outer space and other planets in everybody.

The project is based on Olympus Mons, a huge mountain on mars, that is arguably the tallest mountain in the Solar System, probably the tallest physical structure on another planet that we know of. It's about 20km tall and 600km wide. You might be surprised considering its width. I mean the mountains I drew in kindergarten were way less wide than they were tall (very pointy triangles in fact). Olympus Mons is also not as steep as a stereotypical mountain. If you stood on Olympus Mons, it would appear as a very smooth, gradual plain slowly increasing in height; it wouldn't even have been hard to walk to the summit Caldera (forgetting the 300km "walk"), and you probably can barely see the slope anyway.

So this game allows you to experience the sheer scale and vastness of Olympus Mons, more than 2 times taller than Mount Everest, and way wider. If you do get surprised by the scale, keep in mind that this model that I made is 1:10 scale because of floating point issues... It would have started glitching if I kept it 1:1 scale... Getting the model was slightly harder than expected... I did have the option to just use Sketchfab, but I never found the right one... I thought it would have been easier to just import a heightmap into Blender and figure it out then. The figuring out part was easy; finding a heightmap was the harder part. But I did end up finding a heightmap and got a model I am happy with.

Please make note that I am a complete beginner; my code might be unorganized, wrong, or just overall unoptimised, and if you have feedback, please do tell me. I am a beginner in Blender too, so do not expect the rover to be fancy; I used an actual concept as a reference and got the version with less than a quarter of the triangles of PS1 3D models...
